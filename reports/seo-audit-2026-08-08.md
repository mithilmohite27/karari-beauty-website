# Karari Beauty SEO Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-08-08

**Production site:** <https://www.kararibeauty.com>

**Repository:** <https://github.com/mithilmohite27/karari-beauty-website>

**Scope:** Technical SEO, indexation controls, on-page metadata, ecommerce schema, local/entity signals, content quality, accessibility/SXO, performance, analytics readiness, and AI-search readiness.

## Executive summary

The storefront had a healthy crawlable foundation, but several template-level defects could send misleading signals to search engines: six public pages canonicalized to the homepage, unknown collection URLs returned soft `200` pages, all products claimed an invented brand and `InStock` status, collection HTML contained duplicate H1 headings, and sitemap timestamps were generated rather than factual.

The safe code-level fixes have been implemented and deployed. The production build passes, and a post-deployment crawl of all 180 live sitemap URLs found:

- zero non-`200` public sitemap URLs;
- zero missing or incorrect canonical URLs;
- zero pages with an invalid H1 count;
- zero duplicate titles;
- zero titles outside 30–60 characters;
- zero descriptions outside 120–160 characters;
- a real `404` for an unknown collection;
- valid WebSite/Store, Product, CollectionPage, and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD;
- `noindex, nofollow` on authentication routes.

The largest remaining opportunity is not another technical tag. It is catalog quality: short product copy, heavily reused imagery, missing product identifiers/brands, a few duplicate product names, and unverified local-business details. Those items require genuine business data and should not be fabricated for SEO.

## Audit limits

The audit crawled all 180 production sitemap URLs before implementation (160 products, 13 collections, and 7 other pages), all 96 URLs emitted by the local build environment after implementation, and all 180 production URLs again after deployment. The local and Vercel catalog data differ, so both environments were checked.

Google Search Console, Google Analytics reporting, CrUX field data, Google Business Profile, and a commercial backlink index were not connected. Consequently, the audit cannot truthfully confirm Google index coverage, clicks/impressions, field INP, conversion attribution, backlink toxicity, or exact local-profile consistency. The `site:` search operator was inconclusive and is not treated as authoritative indexation evidence.

## Structured-data review

Post-fix templates produce the following graph:

- Homepage: `WebSite` linked to `Store` / `OnlineStore` with stable `@id`, URL, contact details, map, address, logo, and return-policy URL.
- Collection: `CollectionPage` / `ItemList` and `BreadcrumbList`.
- Product: `Product` and `BreadcrumbList`; SKU, category, image, price offer, and availability are emitted only when the underlying value is valid.
- Product offers require a positive numeric price. Unverified brand, rating, GTIN, MPN, and inventory claims are not fabricated.

This follows Google's guidance for [product structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product) and [merchant listings](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing). Rich-result eligibility is never guaranteed and should be checked after deployment in Rich Results Test and Search Console.

## Technical SEO and indexation

- Production `robots.txt` and sitemap were reachable and all 180 pre-fix sitemap URLs returned `200`.
- The sitemap uses the canonical `https://www.kararibeauty.com` host.
- HTTPS, HSTS, major security headers, and normal/Googlebot rendered-content parity were healthy.
- No mixed-content requests were found.
- Unknown collection URLs now return `404`; they no longer look like valid indexed pages.
- Private authentication/admin areas have targeted crawl and index controls without blocking public assets.
- Canonicals are now self-referential across public page types, matching Google's [canonicalization guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls).
- Sitemap dates now reflect known update dates and unknown dates are omitted, consistent with Google's [sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap).

## On-page, content, and SXO

Post-fix metadata is centralized and produces unique, readable page titles and descriptions within the audit's target ranges. Public templates have one descriptive H1, labelled breadcrumbs, better category alt text, complete footer navigation, direct collection CTAs, and an accessible path from Quick View to the canonical product page.

Content remains the main growth constraint. Priority content work should be based on real merchandise knowledge:

1. Write unique product descriptions with materials, size, finish, included items, care, recipient/occasion, shipping constraints, and accurate differentiators.
2. Add genuine product photos with consistent angles and close-ups; avoid a single stock image across a category.
3. Expand collection introductions with useful selection guidance and internal links rather than keyword filler.
4. Add a factual About/brand story and sourcing/service information that demonstrates first-hand experience.
5. Collect and publish verified reviews only through a real review workflow.

Google may generate title links and snippets from several page signals, so concise metadata should accompany—not replace—clear page content. See Google's guidance for [title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) and [snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet).

## AI-search readiness

No separate AI-only implementation is required. Major crawlers can access the public site, public pages are server-rendered, canonicals are consistent, structured data matches visible content more closely, and entity/store information is linked. Google's current guidance states that the same technical and content foundations used for Search apply to AI features; no special AI text file or schema is required. See [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) and the [AI search optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).
